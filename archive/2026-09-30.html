New benchmark tracks whether Claude models get quietly 'nerfed' post-launch

Hacker News (best) Tue 3:36 PM 12 min read ●●●●●

A GitHub project called livenerf runs a daily, pre-registered benchmark on Claude Opus 5.5 (launched Sept 22) to statistically detect if the model's performance degrades after release, addressing recurring community complaints.

Why it matters: Addresses AI industry trust concerns about silent quality degradation in widely-used models.

Key points
  • Uses a 78-question calibrated panel from GPQA/MMLU-Pro/math; measures accuracy and output-token drift daily for 30 days
  • Requires a 99% confidence change of at least 3 points across two 10-day windows, confirmed by a control arm, to call a real change
  • Early finding: lower 'effort' settings cut tokens sharply (-62%) with modest accuracy drop (-8.3 points)
